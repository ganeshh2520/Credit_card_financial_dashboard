# Credit_card_financial_dashboard
Power Bi dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze credit card transactions and customer behavior. The dashboard helps identify revenue trends, customer demographics, spending habits, and key business performance metrics.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- DAX
- Power Query

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

### Transaction Dashboard
- Total Revenue: 56.5M+
- Total Transaction Amount: 45.5M+
- Total Interest Earned: 7.98M
- Total Transaction Count: 667K
- Quarterly Revenue Analysis
- Revenue by Card Category
- Revenue by Expenditure Type
- Revenue by Education Level
- Revenue by Occupation
- Revenue by Payment Method (Swipe, Chip, Online)

### Customer Dashboard
- Revenue by Age Group
- Revenue by Gender
- Revenue by Income Group
- Revenue by Education
- Revenue by Marital Status
- Revenue by Dependents
- Top 5 States by Revenue
- Weekly Revenue Trend
- Customer Satisfaction Score (CSS)

## 📈 Key Insights
- Blue card holders generated the highest revenue.
- Bills and Entertainment were among the highest spending categories.
- Business professionals contributed the largest share of revenue.
- Customers aged 40–60 generated the highest revenue.
- Texas, New York, and California were among the top revenue-generating states.
- Swipe transactions accounted for the majority of revenue.
